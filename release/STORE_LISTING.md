**Title:** Puffy: Deep Sea Adventure

**Short description (1 line):**
Tap to puff up and dive! Dodge urchins, jellyfish and fishing hooks, collect pearls and discover the creatures of the deep sea.

**Description:**
Puffy the pufferfish is going on a deep-sea adventure! Tap to puff up and float, tap again to deflate and sink — a simple one-touch control that's easy to learn and hard to master.

Dive from sunny shallow reefs into the glowing Midnight Zone. Dodge sea urchins, electric jellyfish, swordfish, fishing hooks, nets and plastic trash, and watch out for the deep trenches!

🗺️ **Adventure** – 20 levels across 4 ocean zones, 3 stars per level and an epic boss at the end of every zone: a Giant Crab, a Hammerhead Shark, a Giant Octopus and a Giant Anglerfish.
♾️ **Free Dive** – an endless dive: beat your depth record, use special skills, swap characters mid-dive and witness rare moments like a passing humpback whale.
🐠 **7 sea characters** – each one floats and sinks differently and has its own abilities: the slim seahorse slips through nets, the octopus jets away in a cloud of ink, the anglerfish lights up the dark…
📖 **Sea Encyclopedia** – discover 8 rare creatures with real ocean facts and unlock a secret character.
🎯 **Daily missions & streaks** – including some delightfully silly ones!

**Controls:**
- Tap / Click / Space – puff up (rise) / deflate (sink)
- X or ⚡ button – special skill (Free Dive)
- C or 🐠 button – swap character (Free Dive)
- P / Esc – pause

**Tags / categories:** Casual, Arcade, One Button, Endless Runner, Underwater, Fish, Ocean, Animals, Cute, Kids, Mobile, Boss

**Languages:** English

**Devices:** Desktop and mobile (landscape)
